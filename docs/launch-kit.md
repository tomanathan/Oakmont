# Oakmont launch kit

Everything here is for you to post or send yourself. Nothing in this file has been posted anywhere.

Each place gets its own short link, so the admin page ("Where accounts come from") shows which ones bring signups and which bring paying students. The pattern is `oakmontsat.com/go/<where>` or `oakmontsat.com/go/<where>/<detail>`. Make up new ones whenever you like; they need no setup.

## The free things worth sharing

Lead with these, not with "sign up". A stranger shares a useful free thing; nobody shares a pricing page.

| What | Link | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| SAT question of the day | `oakmontsat.com/daily` | TikTok, Instagram, group chats, tutors' sites |
| 29 free skill pages | `oakmontsat.com/sat` | Reddit answers, counselors, teachers |
| Study plans by test date and length | `oakmontsat.com/sat-study-plan` | Parents, counselors, "how do I start?" questions |
| SAT test dates and countdown | `oakmontsat.com/sat-test-dates` | Parents, school newsletters |
| The five-question starter | `oakmontsat.com/start` | Anyone ready to try it |
| A sample of the parent report | `oakmontsat.com/parents/sample` | Parents |

## 1. TikTok and Instagram (students)

Your "feeding my dog SAT questions" series and the daily question are now the same thing: each day's video can end by sending people to answer it themselves.

- **Bio:** `one SAT question a day. feed the dog. oakmontsat.com/go/tiktok`
- **End card / pinned comment on each daily video:** `today's question is live. answer it and see if you beat my time: oakmontsat.com/daily`
- **Format ideas that use what already exists:**
  - "can you beat Ozho": show the question, a 10-second timer, then the answer. The daily page shows each person their own time.
  - "the trap in this question": one wrong answer and why people pick it. Every question in the app has these written already.
  - "streak check": post your own daily streak from the page and ask for theirs. The share button gives a result line with no spoilers.
  - "send this to the friend who…": the invite link gives both of them a free month.
- **Cadence:** one a day is enough. Post the question video in the morning, when the new question goes up.

## 2. Parents

Parents are the ones who pay, and what they want to know is whether the studying is actually happening.

**Post for a parent group (Facebook, Nextdoor, PTA, WhatsApp):**

> If your junior is taking the SAT this spring: I built a prep site called Oakmont that gives the student a week-by-week plan to their test date and gives the parent a report of what actually got done, so you don't have to ask. The first week is free with no card. There's a sample of the parent report here if you want to see what you'd get: oakmontsat.com/parents/sample
>
> There are also free study plans by test date, whether or not you ever sign up: oakmontsat.com/sat-study-plan

Change "I built" to whatever is true for the person posting. Post as yourself, say plainly that it's yours, and answer questions in the comments. Check each group's rules about promotion first.

## 3. Counselors and teachers

One counselor who mentions it reaches a whole class. Ask for nothing but a look, and give them something they can use without signing anyone up.

**Email to a school counselor:**

> Subject: Free SAT study plans and practice for your juniors
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I run Oakmont, an online SAT prep course. I'm writing because we publish a few things that are free to use and might be handy for your students this spring:
>
> - Week-by-week study plans for each test date: oakmontsat.com/sat-study-plan (they print cleanly)
> - A page for each of the 29 skills on the digital SAT, each with a worked example and practice questions: oakmontsat.com/sat
> - One new practice question a day: oakmontsat.com/daily
>
> None of those need an account. The full course is paid, with a free first week, and it includes a progress report for parents.
>
> If you think any of this would help, you're welcome to link it or pass it along: oakmontsat.com/go/counselor/[school]
>
> Thanks for your time,
> [Your name]

Send these one at a time, to people at specific schools, from your own address. Don't bulk-send.

## 4. Tutors and school websites

Tutors need material, and their sites are exactly the kind of place a link to you helps in search.

- Offer the daily question widget: `oakmontsat.com/daily/embed` has a copy-paste snippet that puts the question of the day on their site, with a link back to Oakmont.
- Offer the skill pages as free homework: "assign `oakmontsat.com/sat/transitions` before our next session."

**Message to a tutor:**

> Hi [Name], I run Oakmont, an SAT prep site. We have a free "SAT question of the day" that can sit on your website with one line of code (oakmontsat.com/daily/embed), and free pages for each of the 29 SAT skills with worked examples that some tutors assign between sessions (oakmontsat.com/sat). Both are free to use. If your students want the full course, this link gives them a free week: oakmontsat.com/go/tutor/[name]

## 5. Reddit and forums

Useful where students ask real questions (r/SAT and similar), and the fastest way to get banned if it reads as advertising. Read each community's rules before posting; many do not allow promotion at all.

- Answer questions properly, in full, in the comment itself. Link a skill page only when it really is the best answer to what was asked ("how do transition questions work?" → the transitions page).
- Say it's your site whenever you link it.
- A post that can stand on its own: "I wrote up every question type on the digital SAT with a worked example for each, free, no signup: oakmontsat.com/sat. Tell me what's wrong or missing." Then fix what they tell you. Critique from that audience is worth more than the clicks.

## 6. Launch posts

For sites where people share things they made (Product Hunt, Hacker News "Show HN", Indie Hackers). The audience is not students, but these bring links, and links are what lift you in search.

> **Oakmont: SAT prep with a week-by-week plan and a report for parents**
>
> I built Oakmont because most SAT prep is a pile of practice questions with no plan. You give it your test date; it builds a week-by-week plan across all 29 skills on the digital SAT, mixes earlier skills back in so they stick, and schedules the official practice tests. Parents get a report of what was actually studied. There's a pixel dog who has to be fed by studying.
>
> Free without an account: a question of the day (oakmontsat.com/daily), a page per skill with worked examples (oakmontsat.com/sat), and study plans by test date (oakmontsat.com/sat-study-plan). The course itself has a free first week, no card.
>
> I'd like feedback on the free pages in particular.

## 7. Search

- Set up Google Search Console for oakmontsat.com and submit `oakmontsat.com/sitemap.xml`. Paste the verification code into Vercel as `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` and redeploy.
- After two or three weeks, look at the "Queries" report. The searches already showing your pages are the ones to write more for.
- Every link from another site helps all of the pages. The counselor, tutor and launch-post steps above are how you get them.

## A simple first two weeks

| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Search Console set up; TikTok and Instagram bios changed to the tracked links |
| 1 to 14 | One daily-question video a day, ending on `oakmontsat.com/daily` |
| 2 | Parent-group post in one group you're actually part of |
| 3 to 5 | Five counselor emails, each written to a specific person |
| 6 to 8 | Five tutor messages offering the widget |
| 9 | One launch post |
| 10 to 14 | Answer ten real questions on Reddit or a forum, in full |
| 14 | Open the admin page: which links brought signups, and which brought students who studied and paid. Do more of whatever worked. |

## What not to do

- No made-up numbers, score promises or testimonials. The site doesn't use them and the posts shouldn't either.
- No posting the same message in many groups. One place, written for that place.
- No asking students to post reviews in exchange for anything.
